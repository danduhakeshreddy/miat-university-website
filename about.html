Meridian Institute of Advanced Technology

Our history, vision, and community

Since 1998

Our History

MIAT began with a simple ambition: make technical education more connected to the questions students want to solve. From an early engineering campus in Chennai, the institute has grown into a multidisciplinary learning community.

Today students learn through studio work, research, industry projects, and a close relationship with faculty. The campus continues to grow around the belief that good engineering begins with listening and careful experimentation.

Where we’re going

Our Vision

We want students to leave MIAT ready to contribute with skill, empathy, and intellectual confidence. A strong education makes room for precise work and generous collaboration.

Our Mission

We connect rigorous fundamentals with practical learning, encourage inquiry across disciplines, and support students as they turn promising questions into useful work.
