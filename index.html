Meridian Institute of Advanced Technology

A place to discover, design, and lead

Meridian Institute of Advanced Technology has shaped engineers, scientists, and entrepreneurs since 1998. Our campus in Chennai brings together world-class laboratories, a research-driven faculty, and a community that values curiosity above all.

Whether you are exploring your first programme, building a start-up, or preparing for a career in industry, MIAT offers the tools, mentors, and opportunities to turn ideas into impact.
