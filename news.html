Campus report · 3 November 2026

MIAT Ranks Among Top Engineering Institutes in South India

The institute has been recognised in a regional education review for its engineering programmes and student support. Faculty members say the recognition reflects steady work across teaching, research, and campus services. Students contributed through project showcases and peer mentoring. MIAT plans to keep investing in laboratories and career guidance while publishing more information about student outcomes.

Student life

Hackathon Draws 800 Students Across Three Days

More than 800 students took part in a three-day campus hackathon focused on practical community challenges. Teams worked with mentors from engineering, design, and business programmes to shape early prototypes. Organisers said the event gave first-time participants a supportive place to test ideas. Several teams will continue refining their projects through the student innovation hub this semester.

Research

New Artificial Intelligence Research Centre Opens on Campus

A new research centre will bring together faculty and students working on responsible artificial intelligence. The centre includes collaborative work areas, computing resources, and weekly seminars. Its first projects explore accessible language tools and methods for evaluating data quality. MIAT says students from different programmes can join through project courses and supervised research placements.

Careers

Placement Season Begins Earlier Than Ever

Career services has begun employer sessions earlier in the academic year to give students more time to prepare. Workshops cover portfolios, technical interviews, and communication. Faculty advisors will coordinate practice sessions with partner companies. The programme is open to students across departments and includes individual feedback so learners can choose next steps that fit their strengths.

Community

Campus Sustainability Drive Plants 2,000 Saplings

Students, staff, and local partners planted 2,000 saplings as part of a long-term campus sustainability drive. The effort focuses on native species and includes a maintenance plan led by student volunteers. Biology and engineering teams will track shade, soil, and water use over time. Organisers hope the project becomes a practical outdoor learning site for the community.

Competition

Engineering Team Wins Inter-College Robotics Trophy

An MIAT student engineering team won an inter-college robotics trophy after demonstrating a compact search-and-rescue prototype. The team combined mechanical design, embedded systems, and careful testing. Judges praised the clear safety features and the students’ explanation of their design choices. Faculty mentors say the project will continue as an open lab activity for new team members.
