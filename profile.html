Student story

Curious about software, committed to people

Arjun studies Computer Science and Engineering at the Meridian Institute of Advanced Technology. He enjoys understanding how reliable software is built and how technology can make everyday tasks easier. He is especially interested in Python and practical problem solving.

He likes breaking a difficult problem into smaller pieces, testing each idea, and learning from the results. Lately, he has been exploring web accessibility and human-centred computing. He studies Computer Science as a way to connect careful theory with useful tools.

Outside class, Arjun works with peers on campus projects, plays chess, and helps organise coding meetups. He believes the best teams make room for different perspectives and careful questions.

His current goal is to build tools that are useful to more people, including people who navigate the web in different ways.
